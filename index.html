How to Apply

  1. Fill out the online application form
  2. Submit required documents
  3. Appear for entrance examination (if applicable)
  4. Attend personal interview
  5. Receive admission offer
  6. Complete enrollment process

Important Dates:

  • Application Deadline: June 30, 2024

  • Results Announcement: July 30, 2024

  • Minimum 50% in qualifying examination
